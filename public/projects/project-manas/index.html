Project MANAS

Autonomous ground and aerial systems work with MIT's official AI and Robotics team.

May 2021 - May 2023 ROSPythonComputer vision

Overview

Project MANAS is the official AI and Robotics team at Manipal Institute of Technology. I contributed to autonomous ground and aerial systems, with work spanning controls, imaging, and competition infrastructure.

Contributions

  • Developed the ROS control stack for a self-driving car and other autonomous robots, with a focus on reliable navigation and system integration.
  • Designed an imaging pipeline and ODCL web application for AUVSI SUAS 2022, supporting manual image classification and automated submission to the interoperability server.
  • Contributed to the UAV flight-stabilisation system.

Outcome

The team ranked 18th of 71 teams globally at AUVSI SUAS 2022, including second in Flight Readiness Review and tenth in Technical Design Review.
