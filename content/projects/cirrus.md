## Overview

Cirrus is a Vulkan-based volumetric rendering exploration focused on the foundations required for cloud rendering.

## What I explored

- Voxel raymarching for traversing and accumulating volumetric density.
- Volumetric light-transport techniques relevant to rendering cloud data.
- A rendering path intended to support experimentation with the Disney Clouds dataset.
